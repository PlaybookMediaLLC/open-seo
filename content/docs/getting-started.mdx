## 1. Sign in

Open [relay.playbookmedia.io](https://relay.playbookmedia.io) and select **Continue with Google**. Relay signs you in with your Google account. It does not store a password.

## 2. Create a project

A project is one website. Enter its domain when Relay asks. You can add more projects later from the project switcher at the top of the sidebar.

## 3. Connect Search Console

Connect [Google Search Console](/docs/google-search-console) so Relay can read your real clicks, impressions, and positions. Access is read-only, and you can disconnect at any time. Google Analytics is optional and works the same way.

## 4. Find your first opportunities

Open **Opportunities** and select **Run detection**. Relay reads your Search Console data and lists the page fixes most likely to win clicks, with a priority score and an effort estimate for each one. Detection uses Search Console only, so it uses no credits.

Review an opportunity, decide what to change, and mark it as reviewed. After you ship the change, track it in **Experiments & Results** to see whether it moved clicks.

## 5. Tell Relay about your business

Open **Context** and describe your site, your goal, your audience, and your competitors. Research, reports, and your AI agent use this context, so their answers fit your business.

## Where to go next

- **Keyword Research** and **Saved Keywords** to find and keep keywords worth writing for.
- **Rank Tracking** to follow the keywords that matter each week.
- **Site Audit** to find crawl issues and slow pages.
- **Prompt Tracking** to see whether AI assistants recommend you. See [AI visibility](/docs/ai-visibility).
- **Agent setup** to [connect your AI agent](/docs/agent-setup).

Paid research uses credits. See [Plans and credits](/docs/plans-and-credits).
