## What Relay does

- **Opportunities.** Relay reads Search Console and ranks the page fixes most likely to win clicks, highest impact first.
- **Experiments.** Ship a change and measure it against a baseline, so you know what actually moved.
- **Research.** Keyword research, domain overviews, backlinks, and live search results.
- **Tracking.** Weekly rank tracking and AI visibility tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini.
- **Reports.** Client-ready reports you can share with one link.
- **Agents.** One MCP address gives your AI agent the same tools, data, and project context.
