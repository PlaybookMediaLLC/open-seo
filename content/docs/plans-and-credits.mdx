Relay is free to start. Paid research uses **credits**, because live keyword, search result, and backlink data costs money for each lookup. Relay shows the cost before you run a paid lookup.

## What is free

- Search Console insights, opportunities, and experiments.
- Your projects, project context, saved keywords, and reports.
- Connection checks from your AI agent, such as listing your projects.

## Top-ups

You can buy extra credits at any time: $1 for 1,000 credits. Top-up credits do not expire while your account is open. Monthly plan credits reset at the start of each billing period.

## Spending limits

A spending limit caps the credits that your organization or one project can commit in a calendar month (UTC). Relay checks the limits before each paid lookup and each AI request, so a refused action costs nothing. Each assistant turn reserves 50 credits until it ends, then counts its real cost.

- **Organization limit:** open **Billing**, then click **Edit limit** in **Spending limit**.
- **Project limit:** open **Project settings → Spending**, then click **Edit limit**.

Only the organization owner can change a limit. Every member can see the limits and this month's spend.

Committed credits are the real cost of finished lookups, plus the estimate of lookups that are still running. At 80% of a limit, Relay shows a banner on the project and sends one notification for the month. At the limit, paid actions stop until the next month or until an owner raises the limit. Scheduled rank checks and AI visibility checks skip and show the reason. Free features keep working.

Spending limits count paid lookups and AI requests from October 17, 2026.

## Billing

Stripe processes payments. Paid plans renew every month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in **Billing**, and you keep paid features until the end of the period. See the [Terms of Service](/terms) for the full rules.
